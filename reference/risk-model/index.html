About this model. Factor weights are derived from meta-analytic effect sizes (Niedhammer, Bertrais & Witt, 2021; Stansfeld & Candy, 2006) and mapped to Job Demand-Control-Support and Effort-Reward Imbalance frameworks. This tool has face validity only — it is a conceptual demonstration for educational purposes only, not a validated predictive instrument. Scores should not be used to make individual employment or clinical decisions.
