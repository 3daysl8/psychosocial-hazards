Psychosocial Hazard Investigation Guide

A free guide for HR practitioners and Health and Safety Representatives in Australia. In development.

It will walk you through identifying, investigating and controlling psychosocial hazards after an incident or report, with an indicative risk score and plain-English explanations along the way. Nothing you type will be stored or sent anywhere.

This is an educational guide, not legal advice. It does not determine legal outcomes, and any score it shows is indicative only.

Content current as at 22 October 2026.
