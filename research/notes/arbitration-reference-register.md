## Remaining extraction limits

Do not retain unverified exact statistics merely because the citation resolves. In particular, exact Willness, Brewin/Ozer and Schmidt table cells, repeated Stansfeld I² values, unsourced bullying-frequency estimates, and generic ISO/COPSOQ scoring claims remain outside publication clearance. For Sojo, the accessible author manuscript gives directly inspectable conflicting values; reconcile the version if retaining exact figures. Missing citations such as Toscanelli/Van Hooff require complete records and claim-level checks if retained.

The licence field in registry metadata is supporting information, not a universal clearance for reproducing questionnaire items, tables or third-party materials. Do not import Argon's blanket licence labels. See the arbitration for verified instrument and regulator terms.
