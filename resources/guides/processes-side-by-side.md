# One-page explainer: hazard management, conduct processes and workers compensation: DRAFT outline

Status: draft, for owner review. Finished PDF built in Phase 9 into `site/downloads/guides/`.

## Purpose

Explain on one page that the three processes answer different questions, run under different rules, and run at the same time. Aimed at HR people who arrive thinking about conduct, and at managers who assume a conduct outcome closes the matter.

## Content source (don't copy text here)

From the `processes` block in `site/js/data/triage.js`: for each process, the question it answers, who leads it, what it looks at, and the key point. Plus the Victorian note that the psychological health regulations don't change workers compensation eligibility.

## Layout

- A4 landscape, three columns (one per process), with a short line above: "One report can start more than one process."
- The hazard management column visually emphasised.
- "Reasonable management action" appears only in the workers compensation column, labelled as a claim test that plays no part in assessing or controlling the hazard.
- Footer: disclaimer, version, current-as-at date, site URL.
