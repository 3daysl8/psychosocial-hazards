# Phase 6: Resources

Date: 2026-10-24

## Goal

A Resources section generated from the evidence register: research references with DOIs and plain-English summaries, the frameworks explained simply, regulator links by jurisdiction, how the rating is calculated, and the known limitations stated plainly. Linked from the score display.

## What was built

- **`#/resources`** (`site/js/views/resources.js`), in the sidebar and on the home page. Sections:
  1. How the indicative rating works: short explanation, links to the live panel on `#/risk` and `docs/scoring-method.md`.
  2. What research says about each hazard: one card per hazard in the selected jurisdiction (16 Victoria, 14 Model WHS), with an evidence-type tag (pooled studies, single study, mostly cross-sectional, prospective not pooled, no pooled estimate found), a plain-English summary with verified figures, and short source names. Plus "When hazards combine" (Lunen 2023).
  3. Frameworks, explained simply: demand-control, demand-control-support, effort-reward imbalance. Marked draft.
  4. Regulator guidance: the SWA Model Code and WHO guidelines always, plus the jurisdiction's guidance list from `jurisdictions.js` (de-duplicated), and a link to Law and regulators.
  5. What the evidence can't tell you: eight limitations.
  6. Research sources: 20 references with DOI links and plain-English summaries.
  7. How the evidence was checked: short note and a link to the published review.
- **`site/js/data/evidence.js`** holds all of it. Each part names its `EVIDENCE_REGISTER.md` section.
- **Score panel** now links to Resources ("The research and guidance behind this rating").
- **Register:** new "Guide hazards: what the research shows" table (the old weight mapping kept, marked superseded); nine sources added from the audited review; Karasek, Johnson & Hall and Siegrist citations VERIFIED via Crossref with DOIs; WHO guideline added (from the owner's PDF); limitations rewritten for the per-hazard rating.
- Version 0.6.0.

## Decisions and why

- **Only VERIFIED figures appear**, each with its outcome and design, and a note that figures can't rank hazards. This follows the review's corrections (A3, A7, A8).
- **Evidence-type tags instead of strength labels**, so a "no pooled estimate" hazard doesn't read as low risk.
- **Blindow 2024 wording narrowed** after reading its abstract: mostly sexual violence and harassment; only two studies on non-sexual gendered violence.
- **The site's data file is a hand-kept mirror of the register**, not generated by a script, to keep the no-build-step rule. The file header says to change both in the same commit.
- **Regulator links reuse the Law page's data**, so they can't drift apart.

## Tested

- Every JS module imports cleanly in Node and the browser. (One unescaped apostrophe broke the page on first load; found by importing each module, fixed.)
- Browser (local server, agent-browser): Victoria shows 16 hazard cards, Model WHS 14; two-author sources read "Stansfeld & Candy 2006" and "Johnson & Hall 1988"; no duplicate SWA Code link under Model WHS; score panel links to `#/resources`; no console errors; no sideways scroll at 390px.
